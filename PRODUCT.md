# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for a single-page demonstration without a backend.

## Users

People considering a salon visit in Ahmedabad who want to review services and contact or locate the salon.

## Product Purpose

Present Elite Style Salon's services, sample reviews, and contact details in a premium salon website demonstration.

## Positioning

No distinct business differentiator was supplied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single-page static experience. No confirmed live booking, payment, CRM, CMS, or email service. Business details, ratings, review counts, and testimonials are fictional demonstration content.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the salon name, service list, sample address, phone number, hours, rating, review distribution, and example reviews. All are fictional and must be labeled as such.

## Product Principles

- Make services and contact information easy to scan.
- Keep fictional information visibly identified as demonstration content.
- Do not imply that booking or other backend services are live.
